High Device CPU & Memory (RAM) Consumption Notice

Direct video uploads are kept purely in your browser's local storage. Processing large video files directly from local storage requires slicing, buffering, and streaming multi-megabyte/gigabyte media blobs in your browser. This heavily consumes your laptop or phone's CPU and RAM.

Recommended for Permanent Storage & Zero Device Lag: To keep your recording permanently preserved and avoid high CPU/RAM usage on your device, upload the video to YouTube (as Unlisted or Private), and paste the URL into the "YouTube Link" tab!

Supported formats: MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, MP3, WAV, M4A.
